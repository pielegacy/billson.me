Just make sure you hav Bolognese, Lasagna, and Tortellini 

1. Trattoria La Montanara - try the chocolate cake!

## Firenze

* Ristorante il Paiolo - the steak looked good, the not steak was good!
* Bella Italia Ristorante-Osteria - excellent place for simple Italian, AI music was bad but still funny, carbonara slobbed my knob
* Antica Pizzeria Rigene - Next to a very popular pizza place that has massive lines, equally as good 'za tho
* Chianti Wine tour was really good
* I' Girone De' Ghiotti - Big line but the sandwiches are really good

## Cinque Terre (Mainly Monterosso)

* Day on the beach in Monterosso is absolutely worth it - Bagni Eden was a good beach bar with nice food
* Buranco is a fun dinner on top of a hill in Monterosso - highly rate and the wine was great
* Enoteca Da Eliseo was a nice little wine bar

## Rome

* Ditrambo
	* Sleeper hit - went here desperate for lunch and had an amazing veal schnitzel and carbonara
	* best meal we had in Rome
* Vineria Risorgimento
	* great wine bar post vatican
* Popeyes Chicken
* Goat Gallery - if you want AFL they will play it
* Mimi e Coco - Potential tourist trap but it was a great meal for a good price 
* Xenia Guest Rooms was amazing accommodation considering price and location
